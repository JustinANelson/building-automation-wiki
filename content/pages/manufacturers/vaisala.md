Vaisala's HVAC product brochure (B211277EN) groups the wall, duct, and outdoor humidity and temperature transmitters and the
CO2 transmitters used in building ventilation. Its transmitters appear in building automation as 4-20 mA, 0-10 V, or
Modbus RTU field devices wired to a DDC controller.

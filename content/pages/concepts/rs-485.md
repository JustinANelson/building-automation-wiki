TIA/EIA-485 is a differential, multi-drop serial interface. Two-wire is the common wiring, with an optional four-wire form (per the Modbus serial line specification). Good practice that the Modbus specification also calls out: terminate the line at both ends (line termination, LT), connect the signal common across all devices, and run a single daisy-chain trunk, not a star.

The same cable can carry different protocols on different trunks, but one trunk carries one protocol at one baud rate. BACnet MS/TP, Modbus RTU, and the Siemens APOGEE Floor Level Network all use RS-485 signaling at the physical level.

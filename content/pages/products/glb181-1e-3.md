## Overview

The **Siemens GLB181.1E/3** is an OpenAir™ compact VAV controller designed for medium-to-large VAV terminal boxes, air handling mixing dampers, and pressure-independent ventilation units requiring up to **10 Nm** (`90 in-lb`) of operating torque. It combines a 10 Nm direct-coupled rotary actuator, an onboard digital **differential pressure transducer** (`0–300 Pa`), and a microprocessor-based **VAV DDC controller** inside a compact housing.

Documented in Siemens basic documentation `A6V10356040` (CE1P3544de) and Modbus data sheet `A6V10631832`, the GLB181 provides double the nominal torque of the 5 Nm GDB181.1E/3 to drive damper areas up to approximately `1.5 m²` (`16 sq ft`).

## Technical Specifications

- **Nominal Torque**: `10 Nm` (`90 in-lb`), stall torque `< 14 Nm`.
- **Nominal Voltage**: `AC 24 V` ($\pm 20\%$) at `50/60 Hz`.
- **Power Consumption**: `1 VA` / `0.5 W` standby holding; `3 VA` / `2.5 W` active running.
- **Running Time**: `150 seconds` for `90°` stroke (constant speed, independent of load).
- **Angle of Rotation**: `90°` nominal (`95°` maximum), mechanically adjustable with a limit stop screw.
- **Differential Pressure Sensor**: High-precision integrated silicon diaphragm sensor connected via two `4 mm` push-on silicone tube nipples (`+` high pressure, `-` low pressure).
- **Control Modes**:
  - `DC 0/2–10 VDC` modulating setpoint input.
  - `3-Point Floating` (tri-state) open/close pulses.
  - `Modbus RTU` on `/MO` model ([S55499-D167](https://sid.siemens.com/v/u/A6V10631832)) over RS-485.
  - Step control (`STP`).
- **Feedback Output Signal (`U`)**: `DC 0/2–10 VDC` output configurable for actual airflow ($V_{\text{act}}$) or actual damper position.
- **Damper Shaft Mounting**: Standard universal clamp accommodating round shafts `8–16 mm` diameter or square shafts `6–12.8 mm` across, with minimum shaft length of `30 mm`.
- **Protection**: IP54 enclosure rating; magnetic hysteresis clutch provides continuous stall and overload protection without mechanical limit switches.
- **Manual Override**: Push-button gear disengagement slider allows manual damper repositioning when de-energized.

## Commissioning and Diagnostics

- **Multicolor LED Indicator**:
  - *Dark*: No operating power.
  - *Green (steady)*: Normal, fault-free operating mode.
  - *Red (flashing)*: Pressure measurement error (tubing reversed or blocked).
  - *Red (steady)*: Internal differential pressure sensor failure.
- **Service Tools**:
  - `AST20`: Handheld service terminal plugging into the dedicated front service port beneath the protective cap.
  - `AST22`: USB-to-PPS2 interface converter connecting a technician's laptop to `ACS931` (OEM configuration tool) or `ACS941` (field service software).

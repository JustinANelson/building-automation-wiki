## Overview

The **Belimo AMB24-3** is a heavy-duty, direct-coupled, non fail-safe rotary actuator designed for on/off and floating-point control of large dampers, central air-handling unit mixing boxes, and multizone bypass dampers requiring up to **20 Nm** (`180 in-lb`) of torque. Documented in Belimo technical data sheet `AMB24-3 • en-us`, the AMB series drives damper surface areas up to approximately `45 sq ft` (`4.2 m²`) with an energy-efficient brushless DC motor.

## Technical Specifications

- **Torque**: `20 Nm` (`180 in-lb`) minimum across the full stroke.
- **Nominal Voltage**: `AC/DC 24 V` at `50/60 Hz` (range: `AC/DC 19.2–28.8 V`).
- **Power Consumption**: `2.5 W` running; `0.5 W` holding in rest position.
- **Transformer Sizing**: `5.5 VA` (Class 2 supply).
- **Running Time**: `95 seconds` for `90°` rotation (constant speed, independent of aerodynamic load).
- **Control Types**:
  - `On/Off` (two-position) binary control.
  - `Floating Point` (3-point / tri-state) dual-relay modulation.
- **Direction of Motion**: Selectable via a reversible `0/1` slide switch on the housing.
- **Angle of Rotation**: Maximum `95°`, adjustable with an integrated mechanical stop.
- **Sound Level**: `45 dB(A)`.
- **Position Indication**: Visual pointer with mechanical dial indicating `0–95°` stroke.
- **Shaft Clamping**: Universal direct-mount V-bolt clamp fitting round shafts from `1/2" to 1.05"` (`12 to 26.7 mm`) diameter and square shafts from `1/2" to 3/4"` (`12 to 19 mm`).
- **Enclosure and Ratings**: NEMA 2 / IP54 (UL Enclosure Type 2), plenum-rated per UL 2043 (NEC Section 300.22(C) and IMC Section 602).
- **Ambient Operating Range**: `-22 to 122 °F` (`-30 to 50 °C`), up to `95% RH` non-condensing.
- **Electrical Connection**: `3 ft` (`1 m`), `18 AWG` plenum-rated cable with `1/2"` NPT conduit fitting (terminal strip on `-T` models).

## Features and Accessories

- **Manual Gear Disengagement**: An external push-button on the cover temporarily disengages the gear train, allowing technicians to manually position large dampers during commissioning or mechanical alignment.
- **Auxiliary Switch Model (`AMB24-3-S`)**: Features an adjustable internal SPDT auxiliary switch (`3 A @ 250 VAC`) for proof-of-closure signaling or safety circuit interlocks.
- **Anti-Rotation Strap**: Shipped with a heavy-gauge zinc-plated steel anti-rotation strap to secure the actuator body against high reaction torques.

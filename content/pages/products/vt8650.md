## Applying the VT8650

This section is an overview drawn from the part-number descriptions above and the other pages in this wiki.

The VT8650 controls packaged rooftop units and heat pumps, from one-stage heating and cooling up to three heat and two cool stages with modulating heat, and includes inputs for CO2 and fresh air. That makes it suitable for demand-controlled ventilation on a small rooftop; see [CO2 sensors](../concept/co2-sensors.html). Humidity sensing allows dehumidification sequences. It also supports user scripting for sequences the built-in logic does not cover, so document any script and keep a backup.

**Variants.** `U5000B` has no PIR; `U5500B` adds a PIR motion sensor; `U5500BP` adds the PIR and on-board Zigbee Pro. All use BACnet MS/TP.

**Checks.** Select the equipment type (rooftop or heat pump) and the stage count, then confirm the reversing valve arrangement for heat pumps in the controller documentation; see [Heat pumps](../concept/heat-pumps.html). Where a rooftop has an economizer, see [Economizer control](../concept/economizer-control.html). For the 7000-series equivalent see [VT7600](vt7600.html).

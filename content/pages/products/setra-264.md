From the Setra data sheet SSP 264 Rev. L (04/2018). Part numbers are assembled from ordering codes rather than listed one by one: 2641 (model), a range code, an output code (11 = 4 to 20 mA, 2D = 0 to 5 V), an electrical termination (T1 terminal strip, A1 1/2 in. conduit enclosure) and an accuracy code (C = 1 %, E = 0.4 %, F = 0.25 %; the optional accuracy codes include a calibration certificate).

| Item | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ranges | Unidirectional 0 to 0.1 in. WC up to 0 to 100 in. WC, and 25 Pa to 1000 Pa; bidirectional plus or minus 0.05 in. WC up to plus or minus 50 in. WC, and plus or minus 25 Pa to 1000 Pa |
| Accuracy | plus or minus 1.0 % FS standard; 0.4 % or 0.25 % optional |
| Long-term stability | 0.5 % FS per year |
| Voltage output | 3-wire, 9 to 30 VDC excitation, 0 to 5 VDC out; 2.5 V at zero pressure on bidirectional ranges |
| Current output | 2-wire 4 to 20 mA, load 0 to 800 ohm; 12 mA at zero on bidirectional ranges |
| Media | Clean air or similar non-conducting gases; 10 PSI maximum line pressure |
| Operating temperature | 0 to 175 degrees F (-18 to 79 degrees C) |
| Fittings | 3/16 in. barbed brass for 1/4 in. tubing; zero and span adjustable on top of case |

Listed applications include room pressurization, VAV and fan control, and lab and fume hood control. The unit is calibrated in the vertical position, so mounting orientation shifts the zero, most on the lowest ranges (2.3 % FS per g at 0.1 in. WC).

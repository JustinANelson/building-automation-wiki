The **Viconics VTR7300 Series (`VTR7350` / `VTR7355`) and VC3000 Series Relay Packs** (Product Catalog `028-6100-13`, May 2025) form a two-component line-voltage (`110–277 VAC`) fan-coil retrofit solution used in older campus dormitories and office buildings where line-voltage fan-speed switches were originally installed at the unit or wall.

### Two-Component Retrofit Architecture
1. **VC3000 Series Universal Relay Pack (`VC3300E5000` to `VC3514E5000`)**: Mounts inside the fan-coil cabinet, accepts universal `110–277 VAC` line power, switches the `High`/`Med`/`Low` line-voltage fan motor windings and line-voltage (or 24 VAC SSR on `VC3514E5000`) zone valves, and incorporates an internal step-down power supply that powers the wall controller over a 3-wire low-voltage cable.
2. **VTR7300 Wall Room Unit (`VTR7350A...` / `VTR7355A...`)**: Mounts on the room wall using only 3 low-voltage wires (`Power`, `Common`, `Data`) to the `VC3000` relay pack—eliminating line-voltage wiring in the wall box while providing **BACnet MS/TP** (`B` suffix) or **Zigbee Pro** (`P` suffix), PIR occupancy sensing (`-5531`), and RH dehumidification (`VTR7355`). The same `VC3000` relay packs are also compatible with touchscreen **VTR8350** wall controllers.

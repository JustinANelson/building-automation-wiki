## Applying the VTR8350

This section is an overview drawn from the part-number descriptions above and the other pages in this wiki.

The VTR8350 is a wall controller for **line-voltage** fan coils (2-pipe or 4-pipe, with ECM fan control). The wall device does not carry line voltage: it works with a unit-mounted VC3000 relay pack that switches the fan and valves, joined by a low-voltage bus. That keeps the thermostat at low voltage and lets it be mounted and wired like any room sensor.

**Variants.** `A5000B` has no PIR; `A5500B` adds a PIR motion sensor; `A5500BP` adds the PIR and has Zigbee Pro on board. All use BACnet MS/TP.

**Checks.** Confirm the relay pack model suits the unit's voltage and loads before ordering, and check the controller's configured pipe arrangement and fan type. For the same application at low voltage, see the [VT8350](vt8350.html); for the earlier series see the [VTR7300](vtr7300.html). Network notes are on [BACnet MS/TP](../protocol/bacnet-mstp.html).

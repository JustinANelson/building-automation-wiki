The **Viconics VT7300 Series** (`VT7300`, `VT7350`, and `VT7355`, Product Catalog `028-6100-13`, May 2025) is Viconics' segment-LCD 24 VAC low-voltage fan-coil room controller family, widely used across campus residence halls, faculty offices, and classrooms.

### Model Naming Key & Capabilities
- **Series Prefix**:
  - `VT7350`: Standard 7-day programmable commercial fan-coil controller (`2-pipe` cooling, heating, or automatic seasonal changeover; `4-pipe` cooling and heating; optional electric reheat).
  - `VT7355`: Adds an onboard **Relative Humidity (`RH`)** sensor and active dehumidification sequence (reheat during cooling).
  - `VT7300F...-ECM`: Provides a `0–10 VDC` modulating output specifically for **Electronically Commutated Motor (`ECM`)** variable-speed fan coils.
- **Valve Control Code**: `C` = 24 VAC 2-position (On/Off, pulsing, or floating 3-wire valves such as **Siemens 599 Series** `SFA71U`/`SSE131.09U`), `F` = `0–10 VDC` modulating valves (`SSE161.05U`).
- **PIR Occupancy & Networking**: `-5031` = no PIR cover; `-5531` = integrated Passive Infrared (`PIR`) occupancy cover for automatic unoccupied setback; suffix `B` = **BACnet MS/TP**; suffix `P` = **Zigbee Pro** wireless.

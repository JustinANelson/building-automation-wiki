The **Phoenix Controls Fume Hood Display 500 Series (`FHD500`)** (Installation Guide `651-000-014 Rev A`) is the fume hood operator interface, alarm annunciator, and local sash/velocity sensor interface in the **Critical Spaces Control Platform (CSCP)**, complying with ANSI/ASSP Z9.5, ASHRAE 110, NFPA 45, and OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1450 laboratory ventilation standards.

### Features & Terminal Connections
- **Operator Display & Safety Alarms**:
  - Backlit LCD showing real-time face velocity (`FPM` or `m/s`) and alarm messages, paired with high-visibility **Green (Normal)**, **Yellow (Caution / High Sash / Standby)**, and **Red (Low/High Face Velocity Emergency)** LED indicators and an `85 dBA @ 4 in.` audible piezoelectric buzzer.
  - Tactile **Alarm Mute**, **Emergency Purge** (commands hood exhaust valve to maximum airflow and triggers audible/visual alarm), and **Night/Energy Setback** pushbuttons.
- **Onboard I/O & Retrofit Compatibility**:
  - **8-Pin Terminal Block**: 24 VAC/DC power (`24VAC`, `COM`), isolated **RS-485 BACnet MS/TP** (`NET+`, `NET-`, `SHLD`) to the hood's **ACM** and room **PBC**, plus **2 Universal I/O (`UIO1`, `UIO2`)**, **2 Universal Inputs (`UI1`, `UI2`)**, and **1 Solid-State Digital Output (`DO`)** for connecting vertical/horizontal sash position sensors, sidewall face velocity sensors, and **ZPS310** operator presence sensors.
  - **Retrofit Plates (`RD1`, `RM1`, `RM3`)**: Stainless steel adapter plates allow direct drop-in replacement of legacy Accel II / Celeris `FHD1xx`, `FHMx00`, `FHMx10`, and `FHMx30` monitors without cutting new holes in existing laboratory fume hood posts.

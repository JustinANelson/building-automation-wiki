## Overview

The **Belimo NMB24-3** is the standard direct-coupled, non fail-safe rotary actuator for mid-size HVAC air control applications requiring up to **10 Nm** (`90 in-lb`) of torque. Documented in Belimo technical data sheet `NMB24-3 • en-us`, it is the most widely deployed actuator for medium VAV terminal boxes, economizer return/outdoor dampers, and zone ventilation dampers up to approximately `22 sq ft` (`2.0 m²`).

The actuator utilizes a sensorless brushless DC motor and an Application-Specific Integrated Circuit (ASIC) with Digital Rotation Sensing (DRS) technology to eliminate mechanical end switches while providing full stall protection.

## Technical Specifications

- **Torque**: `10 Nm` (`90 in-lb`) minimum across the full stroke.
- **Nominal Voltage**: `AC/DC 24 V` at `50/60 Hz` (range: `AC 19.2–28.8 V` / `DC 21.6–28.8 V`).
- **Power Consumption**: `2.0 W` running; `0.2 W` holding in rest position.
- **Transformer Sizing**: `4 VA` (Class 2 supply).
- **Running Time**: `95 seconds` for `90°` rotation (constant speed, independent of aerodynamic load).
- **Control Types**:
  - `On/Off` (two-position) binary control.
  - `Floating Point` (3-point / tri-state) dual-relay modulation.
- **Direction of Motion**: Selectable via a reversible `0/1` slide switch on the housing.
- **Angle of Rotation**: Maximum `95°`, adjustable with an integrated mechanical stop.
- **Sound Level**: `45 dB(A)`.
- **Position Indication**: Visual pointer with mechanical dial indicating `0–95°` stroke.
- **Shaft Clamping**: Universal direct-mount V-bolt clamp fitting round shafts from `1/4" to 5/8"` (`6 to 16 mm`) diameter and square shafts from `1/4" to 7/16"`. An optional accessory clamp accommodates shafts up to `3/4"` (`20 mm`).
- **Enclosure and Ratings**: NEMA 2 / IP54 (UL Enclosure Type 2), plenum-rated per UL 2043 (NEC Section 300.22(C) and IMC Section 602).
- **Ambient Operating Range**: `-22 to 122 °F` (`-30 to 50 °C`), up to `95% RH` non-condensing.
- **Electrical Connection**: `3 ft` (`1 m`), `18 AWG` plenum-rated cable with `1/2"` NPT conduit fitting (terminal strip on `-T` models).

## Features and Accessories

- **Manual Gear Disengagement**: An external push-button on the cover temporarily disengages the gear train, allowing technicians to manually position the damper during commissioning or power outages.
- **Auxiliary Switch Model (`NMB24-3-S`)**: Features an adjustable internal SPDT auxiliary switch (`3 A @ 250 VAC`) for proof-of-closure signaling or fan interlocks.
- **Anti-Rotation Strap**: Shipped with a zinc-plated steel anti-rotation strap to secure the actuator body against torsion while permitting thermal expansion.

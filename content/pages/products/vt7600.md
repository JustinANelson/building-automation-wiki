The **Viconics VT7600 Series** (`VT7652`, `VT7656`, `VT7657`, Product Catalog `028-6100-13`, May 2025) is Viconics' segment-LCD wall controller family for packaged rooftop units (RTUs), air-source and water-source heat pumps (`WSHP`), and single-zone air handlers.

### Application Variants
- **VT7652B (`2H/2C + Economizer`)**: Controls 2 cooling stages (`Y1`, `Y2`), 2 heating stages (`W1`, `W2`), supply fan (`G`), and a `0–10 VDC` outdoor air economizer damper for free cooling.
- **VT7656B / VT7656E (`Humidity + Rooftop / Heat Pump`)**: Adds an internal **Relative Humidity (`RH`)** sensor and active dehumidification/humidification control for packaged rooftops (`B`) or `3H/2C` heat pumps (`E`).
- **VT7657B (`IAQ / Demand-Controlled Ventilation`)**: Accepts a `0–10 VDC` `CO2` transmitter input to modulate the outdoor air economizer damper based on space `CO2` concentration alongside free-cooling enthalpy/temperature logic.
- **VT7652W (`Water-Source Heat Pump`)**: Dedicated WSHP sequence with reversing valve (`O/B`), waterside isolation valve control, and high/low pressure and condensate overflow fault inputs.

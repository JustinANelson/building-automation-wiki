## Applying the UC400

This section is an overview drawn from the part-number descriptions above and the other pages in this wiki.

The Tracer UC400 is a multi-purpose BACnet MS/TP programmable controller, commonly used on fan coils, blower coils, small air handlers and water-source heat pumps. Only the first order number on this page is confirmed from a manual; the others are unverified, as the descriptions say, so confirm any variant with Trane before ordering.

**Using it.** It can run factory profiles or custom logic built in Trane's graphical programming tool; use a profile where one fits, and document and back up any custom program. It is commissioned with [Tracer TU](tracer-tu.html). Its water-source heat pump sequences, including backup heat, coil icing protection and waterside economizer, are summarised on [Heat pumps](../concept/heat-pumps.html).

**Network.** It sits on a BACnet MS/TP trunk under a supervisory controller such as the [Tracer SC+](tracer-sc-plus.html); set a unique address and match the baud rate. See [BACnet MS/TP](../protocol/bacnet-mstp.html) and [RS-485](../concept/rs-485.html).

**New work.** For new projects Trane's [Symbio](../product-line/trane-symbio.html) controllers are the current family, with the UC series described on [Tracer UC](../product-line/trane-tracer-uc.html). The two can coexist on one system.

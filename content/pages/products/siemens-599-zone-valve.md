The **Siemens Acvatix 599 Series Zone Valves** (`599-00210` to `599-00233`, Technical Bulletin `155-291` / `TB254` `A6V11547593`) and **SFA / SSE / SSF** actuators are short-stroke (`1/10 in.` / `2.5 mm`) brass terminal unit valves used across campus buildings on VAV hot-water reheat coils, 2-pipe and 4-pipe fan-coil units, chilled beams, and radiant panels.

### Valve Body & Actuator Matrix
- **Valve Specifications**: ANSI Class 250 forged brass body (`C37700`), stainless steel stem, EPDM O-rings, fluid temperature `34 to 230 °F` (`1 to 110 °C`), up to 50% propylene/ethylene glycol, close-off pressure ratings from `20 psi` (`1 in.`) to `75 psi` (`1/2 in. 1.0 Cv`).
- **Actuator Options & Assembly Prefixes**:
  - **241-xxxxx**: `SFA11U` (120 VAC 2-position spring-return Normally Closed) + `599-xxxxx` valve body.
  - **242-xxxxx**: `SFA71U` (24 VAC/DC 2-position spring-return Normally Closed) + `599-xxxxx` valve body.
  - **244-xxxxx**: `SSE131.09U` (24 VAC 3-position floating fail-in-place, driven by triac outputs on **APOGEE PTEC/ATEC**, **Desigo DXR2**, or **Viconics VT7300/VT7200**) + `599-xxxxx` valve body.
  - **245-xxxxx**: `SSE161.05U` (24 VAC/DC `0–10 VDC` modulating Normally Closed) + `599-xxxxx` valve body.
  - **246-xxxxx**: `SSF161.05U` (24 VAC/DC `0–10 VDC` modulating Normally Open — frequently specified on perimeter VAV hot-water reheat coils for freeze protection) + `599-xxxxx` valve body.

## Applying the VT8350

The **VT8350** is a color-touchscreen room controller designed for low-voltage (24 VAC / VDC) terminal equipment, including fan coil units (FCUs), [chilled beams](../concept/chilled-beams.html), induction units, and radiant hydronic heating/cooling zones. Operating as an application-specific terminal controller with built-in relative humidity sensing, it combines room sensing, user interaction, and DDC loop execution in a single wall-mounted housing.

### Hardware Features & Models

- **Chilled Beam & Radiant Zone Support**: Configurable for 2-pipe and 4-pipe hydronic systems with modulating 0–10 V or floating thermal actuators, implementing dry cooling sequences to maintain supply water temperatures above zone dew point.
- **Condensation & Leak Protection**: Configurable binary inputs accept dry contact signals from pipe-clamped [condensation detectors](../concept/condensation-detectors.html) or floor water leak sensors to immediately force cooling valves closed during condensation alarm events.
- **Custom Lua Scripting**: On-board programmable control sequences permit complex mixed-mode interlocks, outdoor reset curves, and customized natural ventilation lockouts directly inside the room unit.
- **Humidity & Dew Point Monitoring**: Integrated solid-state relative humidity sensor supports active dehumidification routines and space dew point calculation to protect ceiling tiles and chilled beam coils.
- **Variants and Sensors**:
  - `VT8350U5000B`: RH sensing and control without passive infrared (PIR) occupancy sensor; supports plug-in Zigbee Pro module.
  - `VT8350U5500B`: Adds integrated PIR motion sensor for automatic occupancy setback.
  - `VT8350U5500BP`: Factory-equipped with on-board Zigbee Pro wireless transceiver.
- **Wireless Sensor Integration**: Zigbee Pro communications support wireless door/window reed contacts, remote temperature sensors, and leak detectors, enabling immediate mechanical cooling lockout when occupants open windows for [natural ventilation](../concept/natural-ventilation.html).

### Communication & System Architecture

- **BACnet MS/TP & Modbus RTU**: Dip-switch and menu-selectable serial fieldbus integration with building automation systems.
- **Wi-Fi BACnet/IP Option**: Optional `VCM8002V5031` adapter provides direct BACnet/IP connectivity and automated email alarm notifications without local RS-485 cabling.

Where terminal units operate on line voltage (100–277 VAC), the companion [VTR8350](vtr8350.html) pairs with a VC3000 relay pack. For legacy installations, the 7000-series predecessor is the [VT7300](vt7300.html).
